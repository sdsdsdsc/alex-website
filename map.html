Additional information

About community records

Community records are contributed and maintained by this local project. Search and Filters apply to these records only. Map categories use asset type first, with the legacy category field as a fallback; unknown values use the fallback symbol.

About official heritage markers

Official source facts are separate from project-produced locations. Filled diamonds are reviewed locations; hollow diamonds are general references. Solid lines and restrained fills identify reviewed line or boundary references; dashed, lighter geometry identifies approximate, generalized, or uncertainty meanings. The popup states the controlled meaning and provenance. Category glyphs and English labels are project presentation aids derived only from validated published Map features. Category choices affect Map visibility only and do not change official source values. Project locations and geometry are not official designation coordinates or legal boundaries.
