Community record classification

Asset Type

Asset Type answers “What kind of place is this?” Heritage Criteria answer “Why does this place matter?”

How Asset Type is used

A published community record normally has one main Asset Type. The category helps visitors filter the Map and Places catalogue; it does not decide whether the place has heritage significance.

Choose the closest type for the whole place. Use the description and evidence to explain any features that overlap categories.

Asset Types

BuildingsStanding buildings and structures.Parks and gardensDesigned or community-valued green spaces.Monuments and landmarksFeatures that commemorate, orient or give identity.Other sites and landscapesPlaces not mainly described by the other three types.

Unknown or uncategorized

Some older or incomplete published records may not yet have a reviewed Asset Type. They appear under the fallback “Unknown or uncategorized” map category until their classification is reviewed. This is a fallback state, not a normal heritage category.
