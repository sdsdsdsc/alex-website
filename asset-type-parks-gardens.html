Asset Type

Parks and gardens

Designed or community-valued green spaces where layout, planting, use or memory is central to the record.

What this type can include

Examples include public parks, gardens, cemeteries, recreation grounds, planted squares, institutional grounds and community-managed green spaces.

Record the boundaries carefully and explain important paths, planting, structures, views or patterns of use.

Asset Type is not significance

Being a green space does not automatically give a place heritage significance. Explain its local history, design, community use, memories, rarity or other value through the Heritage Criteria.

Consider when nominating

  • Who created or cared for the space, and how has it changed?
  • Which layouts, plants, structures, views or activities are important?
  • How does the community use or remember the place?
  • What maps, photographs, plans or local accounts provide evidence?
