Community heritage guidance

About Local Heritage Records

Alex's Photo Board documents community-valued places, local stories, and open heritage data so people can understand what matters locally and why.

What this project records

The site records community-valued places and local heritage assets. These may include public spaces, buildings, parks, streets, routes, landmarks, public art, industrial heritage, and places connected to local memory.

The central record layer is Community Places, stored in the site's communityPlaces collection.

What a community heritage record means

A community heritage record describes a place and explains why it matters locally. A record may include its location, asset type, local significance, heritage criteria, condition, community use, source references, and related stories.

Bringing factual information together with local meaning helps visitors understand how a place contributes to community identity and memory.

Our community heritage criteria

The project currently uses the following criteria to structure local significance and supporting evidence:

  • Historic interest
  • Social or communal value
  • Landmark or streetscape value
  • Architectural, design or artistic interest
  • Archaeological or evidential interest
  • Rarity
  • Group value
  • Age
  • Condition or vulnerability

These are community heritage criteria, not official statutory designation criteria. They provide a consistent way to describe local significance and the evidence that supports it.

How the website is organised

PlacesBrowse and search community place records. MapExplore the same records by location. Local History StoriesRead stories about local places, people, and memories. Community NewsFollow current community and heritage activity. Open DataDownload reusable public heritage records.

How places and stories connect

Place records can link to related news and history articles, while articles can link back to the places they discuss. These two-way links create a simple connected heritage graph that lets visitors move between structured records and local stories.

Open heritage data

The Open Data page generates heritage.json, which exports public records as JSON-LD. This structured format can help other websites and research tools reuse the data, and may support future RDF or triplestore work.

The export currently focuses on Community Places, Community News, and Local History Stories.

Future nominations

A future phase may allow people to suggest or nominate community-valued places. Nominations would go through review before becoming published Community Places records.

Public submissions should remain separate from published records and must not directly overwrite them.

Important note

Alex's Photo Board is a community heritage record project inspired by Local Heritage Listing practice. It is not an official statutory Local Heritage List and does not represent a planning authority.
