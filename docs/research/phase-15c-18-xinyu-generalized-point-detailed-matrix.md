## P04 bounded multi-datum and support calculation

### Source and plausible interpretations

The archived Jiangxi Provincial People's Government protection-range annex
states:

> 以GPS（北纬27°45′45.3″，东经114°55′11.2″）为中心，向东、南、西、北四面方向各延伸30米。

The original is preserved as latitude then longitude. Arithmetic transcription
is `[114.91977777777778, 27.762583333333332]`.

The finite interpretation set is:

1. WGS84 geographic longitude/latitude, because GPS broadcast positions use a
   WGS84 reference frame; and
2. CGCS2000 geographic longitude/latitude, because CGCS2000 was China's formal
   national geocentric frame before the 2019 annex and official Chinese GPS/GNSS
   control products are published in that frame.

This is a bounded interpretation set, not an assertion that the source author
used either named datum. GCJ-02 and BD-09 are excluded because they are
commercial web-map display systems, not plausible survey-GPS datums for the
annex context. Beijing 1954 and Xi'an 1980 are excluded because the source
expressly labels geographic DMS from GPS and provides no legacy projected-grid
notation or conversion context.

For this map-scale decision, the CGCS2000 interpretation uses the same
geographic numerical values as the WGS84 interpretation and carries a
conservative `1 m` frame/transformation envelope. This does not claim survey
equivalence. Published geodetic literature records decimetre-scale realization
differences, while the policy decision rounds outward rather than sharpening
the source.

| Accepted interpretation | Reproducible WGS84 treatment | WGS84 result | Nominal separation from WGS84-literal result | Accepted separation bound |
| --- | --- | --- | ---: | ---: |
| WGS84 geographic DMS | DMS-to-decimal identity conversion | `[114.91977777777778, 27.762583333333332]` | `0 m` | DMS precision handled separately |
| CGCS2000 geographic DMS | Preserve the geographic numerals at map precision and apply the documented outward frame allowance; no unsupported survey parameters are invented | `[114.91977777777778, 27.762583333333332]` at retained precision | `0 m` at retained precision | `1 m` |

Thus the two nominal interpretation centres coincide at the retained precision;
the complete accepted inter-interpretation separation is bounded by the
outward `1 m` allowance. A later source-specific transformation may replace
this conservative rule, but cannot silently sharpen it.

### Reproducible calculation

| Quantity | Value and rule |
| --- | --- |
| Original coordinate | `27°45′45.3″ N, 114°55′11.2″ E` |
| Literal decimal interpretation | `[114.91977777777778, 27.762583333333332]` |
| DMS half-unit precision | about `1.37 m` longitude and `1.55 m` latitude |
| Source-described support | 30 m in each cardinal direction; 60 × 60 m semantic support square |
| Farthest support corner including DMS half-unit precision | `44.49 m` from the literal centre |
| Datum/transformation envelope | `1 m`, applied outward |
| Deterministic representative rule | Centre of the two accepted interpretation envelopes; they coincide at retained map precision |
| Deliberate display rounding | Round longitude and latitude to four decimal places |
| Rounded representative | `[114.9198, 27.7626]` |
| Rounding displacement | `2.87 m` from the literal transcription |
| Final outward coverage | `ceil(44.49 + 1 + 2.87) = 49 m`; publish/review as `50 m` support coverage |
| Final displayed precision | Four decimal degrees; approximately 10 m class, not survey precision |

The `50 m` value is the distance required for the rounded representative to
cover the complete accepted support envelope. It is not a confidence interval,
site radius, excavation extent, or legal boundary. Source precision,
datum/transformation allowance, deliberate rounding and support extent remain
separate inputs.

### Scale, usefulness and supersession

- At regional and neighbourhood scales the hollow diamond provides a useful
  named vicinity reference.
- At site scale, the symbol and persistent wording must prevent users from
  treating the rounded coordinate as the exact feature or an entrance.
- The rejected square and uncertainty-area prototypes remain rejected because
  they visually imply an archaeological or legal extent.
- A later datum-certified ordinary Point or approved area supersedes this
  Generalized Point. The two must not be active simultaneously.
