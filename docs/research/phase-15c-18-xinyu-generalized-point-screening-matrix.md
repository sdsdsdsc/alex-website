# Phase 15C-18 — Xinyu Generalized reference Point screening matrix

## Purpose and controls

This non-production matrix records the individual Stage 1 reassessment of all
55 Xinyu Official Heritage identities that remain unpublished after PR #69.
It applies the controlling
[Phase 15C-17 R1–R13 policy](../policy/phase-15c-17-generalized-point-policy-recalibration.md)
without copying the Phase 15C-16 category outcomes. It publishes no coordinate
or feature and is not an application input.

Status codes in the `R1–R13` column are ordered from R1 through R13:

- `P` — Pass;
- `C` — Conditional;
- `F` — Fail; and
- `N` — Not applicable at this screening stage.

The full requirement names and candidate-specific Stage 2 reasons are in the
[canonical audit](../audits/phase-15c-18-xinyu-generalized-point-reassessment.md)
and [detailed matrix](./phase-15c-18-xinyu-generalized-point-detailed-matrix.md).
`C` in the outcome column means the Generalized Point route is unsuitable, not
that the identity itself is unsuitable for every future representation.
