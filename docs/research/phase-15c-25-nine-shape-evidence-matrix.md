## Scope and outcome model

This research-only matrix investigates exactly the nine Phase 15C-24 Outcome B
area candidates at baseline `a178a682699708e08fb5944bb4effc46be468962`.
Each appears exactly once. No geometry is approved or published.

`A` means every material shape-research gate is satisfied; `B` means a shape
remains plausible but at least one named material gate is missing; `C` means a
shape route is no longer appropriate; and `D` means withhold. The final result
is **0 A / 9 B / 0 C / 0 D**.
