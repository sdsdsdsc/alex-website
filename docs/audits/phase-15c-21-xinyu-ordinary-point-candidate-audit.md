ordinary Point** by Phase 15C-18. It finds:

- **0 Outcome A** — no ordinary Point publication-ready candidate;
- **11 Outcome B** — N03, N04, N08, P05, P07, P11, P20, P22, M13, M23 and

future publication batch. All B and D identities remain operationally withheld
until candidate-specific evidence and approval gates pass. No coordinate,

## Method and evidence rules

The review date is `2026-08-08`. The evidence reviewer is **Codex
documentation research agent**; publication authority remains the **Alex's
Photo Board project owner**.

For every identity the review:

1. reconciled the Phase 15C-10 official inventory, provider matrix and
   parent/component distinctions;
2. retained the Phase 15C-11 and candidate-specific Phase 15C-13 findings;
3. checked later institutional and public evidence where it could clarify
   identity, physical feature, visitor meaning or locality;
4. rejected map view centres, search-result pins, nearby facilities, villages,
   memorial substitutes and parent/sibling coordinates as numerical evidence;
5. required any Gaode value to remain labelled GCJ-02 and any Baidu value to
   remain labelled BD-09 until a documented conversion and reconciliation
   produces WGS84; and
6. required identity, numerical construction, uncertainty, Point meaning,
   sensitivity, misleading-risk and accountable publication review to pass
   separately.

No fresh source supplied a reusable candidate-specific WGS84 coordinate or a
permitted raw coordinate with a complete CRS and transformation chain. The one
recorded N03 Gaode number remains rejected because it refers to the managed
visitor-complex address rather than a safely isolated historic component.
Consequently no uncertainty radius can truthfully be assigned to any candidate.
