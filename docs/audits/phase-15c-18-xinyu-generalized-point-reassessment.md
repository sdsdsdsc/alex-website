## Method for reopening all previous exclusions

Each identity was reconsidered in this order:

1. re-establish the official identity, level, locality and parent/component
   treatment from the 62-row inventory;
2. reread its individual Phase 15C-10 provider and non-Point evidence and any
   later candidate-specific audit;
3. test whether the evidence defines a site, bounded vicinity, settlement,
   broad administration, or unbounded/conflicting locality;
4. test every genuinely plausible permitted or conditional construction
   method rather than selecting a convenient coordinate;
5. assess whether a designation-level hollow diamond would be useful and
   non-misleading at overview and detail scale;
6. prefer the ordinary Point, line/area or withholding route where one
   generalized Point would hide an evidence gap or distort natural form; and
7. include uncertain but plausibly boundable candidates in Stage 2.

### P04 Xieli — Outcome A

P04 is the only evidence-eligible candidate under the recalibrated policy.

The official protection-range annex links the exact identity to a DMS `GPS`
centre and a closed rule extending 30 m in each cardinal direction. Xinyu
Museum independently confirms the same approximately 5,000 m² archaeological
identity in Xieli Village. Phase 15C-7 already demonstrated that an area
display would misleadingly imply an archaeological footprint or legal
boundary and that a Generalized Point is the least misleading representation.

Phase 15C-17 makes two previously unavailable decisions controlling:

1. a documented bounded support area may be the spatial basis; and
2. an unstated datum may proceed through a justified finite interpretation
   envelope rather than an unexplained universal uncertainty.

The source context supports WGS84 and CGCS2000 as the finite plausible
geodetic interpretations. GPS broadcast processing uses WGS84; CGCS2000 was
the formal Chinese national geocentric frame before the 2019 annex and is used
for official GPS/GNSS control products. GCJ-02 and BD-09 are not plausible
survey-GPS datums for this government annex and are excluded rather than
automatically stress-tested as source candidates. No source datum is asserted.

The reproducible result is:

- original DMS: `27°45′45.3″ N, 114°55′11.2″ E`;
- literal decimal: `[114.91977777777778, 27.762583333333332]`;
- source support: 60 × 60 m cardinal square;
- half-unit DMS precision: about 1.37 m longitude and 1.55 m latitude;
- conservative datum/transformation allowance: 1 m;
- deterministic four-decimal representative: `[114.9198, 27.7626]`;
- deliberate rounding displacement: 2.87 m; and
- outward support coverage: 50 m.

The 50 m value covers the accepted support envelope from the rounded marker.
It is not an archaeological extent, legal boundary, site radius, source error
or confidence interval. Those concepts remain separate.

P04 passes scale/usefulness review because the 60 m vicinity is limited and
useful, the hollow diamond avoids an invented boundary, and the source already
publishes the centre. It passes sensitivity review at research level because
no excavation, grave, entrance or footprint is added, while final
machine-readable publication still requires owner approval. It passes
misleading-risk review only with persistent candidate-specific wording:

> Generalized reference location for the source-described Xieli vicinity.
> Constructed from the published GPS centre and four 30 m offsets. It does not
> show the exact feature, excavation, grave, entrance, site centre,
> archaeological extent or legal protection boundary.

Any later datum-certified ordinary Point or approved area supersedes the
Generalized Point; simultaneous display is prohibited.

## Outcome C routes

### Ordinary Point route — 25

M13, M14, M15, M16, M17, M22, M23, M27, M29, M30, M32`.

### Multipart/future extent route — 3

- N05 and N06 each contain two named subfeatures. Resolve separate anchors or
  a defensible shared extent rather than inventing one centre.
- M11 is a tomb group. Preserve the future MultiPolygon/group-evidence route
  and withhold individual grave locations without sensitivity approval.

### Future line route — 7

`M18, M19, M20, M21, M24, M25, M26`.

Each bridge requires surviving-fabric identification and a measured or
authoritative alignment. Village, road-crossing, cultural-venue and provider
label centres remain prohibited substitutes. A later reviewed bridge Point may
be independently useful, but a generic vicinity marker would misstate the
protected bridge.

### Future battlefield area — 1

P16 is a roughly 3 km × 100 m dispersed battlefield between named terrain
points. Retain the future elongated Polygon/area route; one Point would
materially collapse the designation and risk substituting a forest park or
memorial.

`25 + 3 + 7 + 1 = 36` Outcome C identities.

## Numerical and implementation contract

P04 demonstrates the actual concepts a later implementation must keep
separate:

- original source coordinate and exact notation;
- source precision;
- plausible datum set and rationale;
- transformation/frame allowance;
- source-described support-area semantics and extent;
- deterministic representative selection;
- deliberate rounding and displacement;
- final displayed precision;
- outward coverage/support value;
- source provenance and limitation provenance;
- persistent popup limitation;
- equivalent accessible-text limitation;
- evidence reviewer/date and accountable publication decision; and
- active representation plus later supersession history.

The current `horizontalUncertaintyMetres`,
`estimatedUncertaintyMeters` and `generalizationRadiusMeters` fields cannot
carry all these concepts without overloading them. Before P04 could be
published, a separate technical PR must extend the existing Phase 15C-1
validation foundation and adapt the existing Phase 15C-2 presentation. It must
not build a new geometry system or publish P04 merely because this audit finds
it evidence eligible.
