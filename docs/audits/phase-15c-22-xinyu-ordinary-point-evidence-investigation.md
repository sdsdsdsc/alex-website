## Method and evidence rules

The review date is `2026-08-09`. The evidence reviewer is **Codex
documentation research agent**; publication authority remains the **Alex's
Photo Board project owner**.

The investigation retained the Phase 15C-10 inventory and provider matrix,
the Phase 15C-11 fallback findings, the Phase 15C-13 candidate matrix, the
Phase 15C-18 route decision and every Phase 15C-21 rejection. Searches then
targeted only the named gaps. Government and heritage-institution evidence was
preferred, followed by reusable OpenStreetMap data and public corroboration.

Search snippets were discovery aids, not numerical evidence. Direct source
pages or data were inspected where available. A Nominatim/OpenStreetMap search
was also run for every exact name and material alias. It returned no usable
candidate-specific object for N03, N04, N08, P05, P07, P11, P20, P22, M23 or
M30. A Taiwan business returned for the P11 name and was rejected. The only
exact reusable object was the M13 building footprint described below.

Gaode values remain GCJ-02 and Baidu values remain BD-09 unless demonstrated
otherwise. No provider value is copied into WGS84 merely by conversion. Map
view centres, villages, entrances, museums, memorials, parents and siblings
remain prohibited substitutes. An ordinary Point must separately pass
identity, numeric construction, CRS, reconciliation, uncertainty, truthful
meaning, misleading-risk, sensitivity/access and accountable-review gates.
No Generalized Point is considered as a fallback.

## M13 numerical reconstruction

The accepted input is the closed WGS84 polygon from OpenStreetMap way
`1255899576`:

```text
[114.9370897, 27.7979463]
[114.9370965, 27.7978286]
[114.9372263, 27.7978345]
[114.9372195, 27.7979522]
[114.9370897, 27.7979463]
```

The project computed the polygon centroid with the standard signed-area
(shoelace) formula after translating coordinates to a local origin to avoid
floating-point cancellation. No datum conversion is applied because
OpenStreetMap geometry is WGS84. The unrounded result is
`[114.9371580, 27.7978904]`; the proposed stored coordinate is
**`[114.937158, 27.797890]`** in GeoJSON longitude/latitude order.

The footprint edges are approximately `13.10 m`, `12.78 m`, `13.10 m` and
`12.78 m`. Its centroid is `28.3 m` from the existing P10 compound-centre
Point `[114.937042, 27.798123]`, while the complete M13 footprint lies inside
the separately named P10 OpenStreetMap compound. This is consistent with the
official locality, the museum's joint visitor management, the existing exact
Gaode/plausible Baidu pavilion matches and historical descriptions placing the
pavilion beside the Confucian Temple. It also establishes the necessary
identity distinction: M13 is the pavilion building; P10's current Point means
the wider temple compound centre.

The recommended horizontal uncertainty is **30 m**. It is derived for this
record, not copied from another marker: roughly 9 m covers centre-to-corner
footprint extent, while the balance conservatively covers unsourced open-map
digitization and cross-source feature-identification error. It does not imply
an official survey or legal boundary. The proposed meaning is
`heritage-building-reference-point`: the centroid of the identified 魁星阁
building footprint, not an entrance, the whole Confucian Temple compound, an
official designation coordinate or a legal protection boundary.

The site is a managed public visitor feature. No burial, archaeological,
private-residence or vulnerable-location concern was found. The principal
misleading risk is confusing the close M13 and P10 markers; future publication
wording must name the pavilion and explicitly state the building-centroid
meaning. At the evidence level, identity confidence is **High**, location
confidence is **Medium**, precision is **approximate**, and the representation
would be a project-reviewed interpretation.

Recommended future wording:

> Approximate project-reviewed reference point at the centroid of the named
> 魁星阁 building footprint. It is not an official designation coordinate,
> entrance, the complete 新余孔庙 compound or a legal protection boundary.
