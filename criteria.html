Community heritage significance

Criteria

Heritage Criteria explain why a place matters locally. Open a criterion to understand the idea and the evidence that can support it.

How Heritage Criteria are used

Criteria describe the stories, design, evidence, community value, rarity or relationships that give a place local significance. A place may meet several Heritage Criteria.

Heritage Criteria answer “Why does this place matter?” They are separate from Asset Type, which answers “What kind of place is it?” Both are community heritage tools, not official statutory designation tests.
