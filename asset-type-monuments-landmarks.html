Asset Type

Monuments and landmarks

Features that commemorate, orient or give identity to a street, neighbourhood, route or landscape.

What this type can include

Examples include memorials, statues, public art, gates, boundary markers, waymarkers, towers and distinctive small structures.

A feature may be visually modest but still act as an important meeting point, marker or focus of local memory.

Asset Type is not significance

Being noticeable or commemorative does not automatically give a feature heritage significance. Explain its local meaning, design, history or relationship to the surrounding place through the Heritage Criteria.

Consider when nominating

  • What does the feature mark, commemorate or help people recognise?
  • Who made, installed or used it?
  • How does its position contribute to a route, view or public space?
  • What inscriptions, images, records or local memories support the story?
