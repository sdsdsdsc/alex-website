Asset Type

Buildings

Standing buildings and roofed or walled structures whose physical form is central to the community record.

What this type can include

Examples include homes, halls, places of worship, shops, schools, bridges, farm buildings, transport buildings and industrial structures.

A group of connected buildings can be described together when the relationship between them is important and the record makes the scope clear.

Asset Type is not significance

Being a building does not automatically give a place heritage significance. Explain why this particular building matters using one or more Heritage Criteria and supporting evidence.

Consider when nominating

  • What is the building's original and present use?
  • Which materials, details, alterations or craft help tell its story?
  • How is it connected to local people, events, work or community life?
  • What photographs, dates, maps or sources support the description?
