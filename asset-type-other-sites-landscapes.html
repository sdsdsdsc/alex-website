Asset Type

Other sites and landscapes

Heritage places that are not mainly a building, park or monument, including routes, working landscapes and archaeological or industrial sites.

What this type can include

Examples include historic routes, archaeological sites, industrial remains, quarries, waterways, field systems, waterfronts and wider cultural landscapes.

Describe what belongs within the place and whether the record concerns a point, route, area, surviving feature or remembered landscape.

Asset Type is not significance

Belonging to this broad type does not automatically give a place heritage significance. Explain the evidence, local history, use, character or memory that makes this particular site important through the Heritage Criteria.

Consider when nominating

  • What survives physically, and what is known mainly through records or memory?
  • What is the clearest way to describe the location and extent?
  • How does the site relate to nearby places, routes, work or landscape change?
  • What maps, photographs, field observations or sources provide evidence?
