Heritage Criterion

Architectural, design or artistic interest

The place has distinctive design, materials, craftsmanship, public art or visual character.

What this criterion covers

Interest may come from overall form, planning, construction, detail, materials, engineering, landscape design or artistic work. It can include skilled local building traditions as well as a known designer or maker.

Examples of useful evidence

  • Clear photographs of form, materials, details and alterations.
  • Plans, drawings, makers' marks or construction records.
  • Information about architects, craftspeople, artists or local traditions.
  • Comparisons that show what is distinctive locally.

Consider when nominating

  • Which features carry the design or artistic interest?
  • What appears original, altered or repaired?
  • Is the workmanship or material locally characteristic?
  • How does the design respond to its use and setting?

Community heritage criterion

This criterion helps explain community heritage value and should not be presented as an official statutory assessment.
