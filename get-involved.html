Community participation

Get involved

Help build a community heritage record by suggesting places, adding local knowledge, and exploring the stories already connected to the map and list.

How you can participate

Alex's Photo Board is building a community heritage record. It is not an official statutory local list, and public submissions do not automatically become official records.

You can nominate a community-valued place, explore existing records, read local stories, and share evidence that helps explain why a place matters.

Nominate a placeSuggest a community-valued place for admin review. Explore the mapUse the map to find places and start a map-based nomination. Search the listBrowse published community place records. Read guidancePrepare a stronger nomination before submitting.

What happens to nominations

Nominations are reviewed before publication. Admin review checks whether the information is clear, whether the place can be identified, and whether the nomination includes useful evidence of local significance.

A submitted nomination is stored separately from published place records. It does not directly create or overwrite a public communityPlaces record.

Private contact information

Your email is used only for admin follow-up about your nomination and is not displayed publicly. Private review notes and admin assessment fields are kept on the admin side.

Please avoid including sensitive personal information in nomination text or evidence fields.

Project position

Alex's Photo Board is a community heritage record project inspired by Local Heritage Listing practice. It is not an official statutory Local Heritage List and does not represent a planning authority.
