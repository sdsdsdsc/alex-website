Use this form to suggest a community-valued place and explain why it matters locally.

Before you begin

Alex's Photo Board is a community heritage record project inspired by Local Heritage Listing practice. It is not an official statutory Local Heritage List and does not represent a planning authority.

  • Nominations will be reviewed before publication.
  • Submitting a form will not automatically publish a place.
  • A nomination does not create an official designation or planning status.
  • Your email will be used only for admin follow-up about this nomination and will not be displayed publicly.

What you can nominate

This form is for suggesting community-valued places such as public spaces, buildings, parks, streets, routes, landmarks, public art, industrial heritage, archaeological features, landscapes, or places connected to local memory.

Please explain the place clearly and provide evidence of its local significance where possible. Do not include private or sensitive personal information.
